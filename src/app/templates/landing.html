Abstract

Steel-truss sizing optimisation under IS 800:2007 is classically solved with evolutionary metaheuristics whose inner loops spend virtually all wall-clock time inside a finite-element solver. This thesis develops an integrated Python framework that composes classical evolutionary optimisation (GA, PSO, NSGA-II) with three modern AI layers — a neural surrogate, a reinforcement-learning agent, and a large-language-model warm-start designer — into one reproducible system, validated against four canonical benchmarks: the 10-bar planar, 25-bar spatial, 72-bar spatial tower, and 200-bar planar stepped tower.

The framework matches Sunar & Belegundu (1991) on 10-bar to 0.004 %; a three-hidden-layer MLP surrogate achieves R2 = 0.9993 on weight and a 132× wall-clock speedup; an LLM warm-start reduces generations-to-convergence on 10-bar by 36.8 % at paired-Wilcoxon p = 0.0046 across 40 seeds; and a rigorous-feasibility re-run of 72-bar exposes that the literature-cited optima are infeasible by 22–62 % under hard-constraint enforcement — a methodological correction to the published literature.

The 8-layer AI stack

L1

Benchmarks

10-bar, 25-bar, 72-bar, 200-bar — pinned geometry, loads, bounds.

L2

FEM kernel

Linear-elastic truss solver, partition-method boundary conditions, O(n3) dense solve (Appendix A).

L3

IS 800:2007 compliance

Clauses 3.8 slenderness, 6.2/6.3 tension, 7.1 Perry-Robertson compression, 5.6.1 deflection (Appendix B).

L4

Metaheuristics

GA (pop 100, gens 500), PSO (swarm 50), NSGA-II (non-dominated sort + crowding) via pymoo.

L5

Neural surrogate

MLP [256,128,64] trained on 10k LHS samples; drop-in FEM replacement with FEM fallback on screen failure.

L6

Reinforcement learning

PPO agent, multiplicative sizing action, 0.2 s inference latency.

L7

LLM warm-start

Claude-Opus / Claude-Sonnet seeds the first population; responses cached for reproducibility.

L8

FastAPI + Streamlit UI

This page + :8501 design aid, sub-10 s end-to-end on 10-bar.
